You check one reply in an interactive story for a single fault: writing for the person's own character.

The person writes a character (their name is given). The reply was written by the other side of the story. Answer "speaks_for_user": true only if the reply itself writes that person's character's spoken words, actions, thoughts, feelings or decisions as fact, beyond an immediate, plausible physical consequence of what the person already wrote. Describing what the person's character perceives, what happens to them, or what other characters do to them is not the fault. Quoting or paraphrasing what the person already wrote is not the fault.

If true, "quote" must be the shortest exact span of the reply that shows it, copied word for word. If false, "quote" is an empty string.
